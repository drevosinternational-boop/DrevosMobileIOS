`error:` line. One unique error was present, repeated twice:

  DrevosIOS/Core/TemperatureUnit.swift:12:16

FILES CHANGED

1. DrevosIOS/Core/TemperatureUnit.swift
   - import Combine
   - TemperatureUnit conforms to Sendable
   - AppSettings is @MainActor-isolated

2. DrevosIOS/Recipes/RecipeService.swift
   - ActiveSmokerStore access remains inside MainActor.run
   - AppSettings and ConnectionMonitor are read together inside MainActor.run

WHY BOTH FILES ARE NECESSARY
----------------------------
Adding @MainActor to AppSettings fixes the reported singleton declaration, but
it also makes the previous direct AppSettings.shared access in RecipeService
illegal. Updating RecipeService prevents that immediate second compiler error.
